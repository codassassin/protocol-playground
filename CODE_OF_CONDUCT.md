# Code of Conduct

Packet City aims to be welcoming to people with different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, constructive and patient.
- Critique technical ideas and evidence, not individuals.
- Welcome questions and beginner contributions.
- Assume good faith and offer actionable feedback.
- Acknowledge others' work.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwelcome sexual attention, doxxing or abusive behavior is not acceptable.

## Reporting
Contact the maintainer through the repository's GitHub contact mechanisms with a description. Reports will be reviewed thoughtfully. This applies to project spaces and interactions related to Packet City.
